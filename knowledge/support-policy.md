# RelayPay Support Policy

**Status:** Source of truth for how RelayPay support handles customer cases
**Version:** 0.1
**Scope:** Personal RelayPay accounts and person-to-person payments

These rules govern support behavior. They do not define product behavior;
product facts live in `product-rules.md`.

## Rules

### SUPPORT-01 — Support never asks for authentication secrets

RelayPay support never asks for a customer's password, PIN, one-time
verification code, or recovery phrase. Support also never asks a customer to
install remote-access software or send money to "verify" an account.

Such requests are not legitimate RelayPay support behavior and should be
reported in the app.

### SUPPORT-02 — Account-specific information requires verification

Support may disclose or discuss account-specific information only after the
customer completes RelayPay's in-app identity verification.

General information about how RelayPay works does not require account
verification.

Support never discloses another customer's account details, balance,
transaction history, or personal data.

### SUPPORT-03 — Unauthorized completed transfers must be escalated as fraud reports

If a customer says they did not authorize a completed transfer, support must
treat it as a fraud report and escalate it to the fraud team. Support must not
handle it as a routine cancellation or reversal request.

### SUPPORT-04 — Support must not predict the outcome of a fraud investigation

Before a fraud investigation is complete, support must not promise a refund,
reversal, or any other outcome.

The possible outcomes of a fraud investigation are not defined in this policy.

### SUPPORT-05 — Support must not promise exceptions to product rules

Support must not promise actions, timelines, refunds, reversals, or other
exceptions that are not supported by the RelayPay product rules.
